Installation Guide

Stone Nav on your iPhone

The app isn't on the App Store. You build it on a Mac and put it on your phone over a cable. No programming experience needed: you only copy the commands below.

What you need

  • A Mac with a macOS version that runs Xcode 27.
  • An iPhone on iOS 27 or later, and a cable for the Mac.
  • An Apple ID, the same one you use for the App Store. A paid developer account isn't required.
  • About 15 GB free for Xcode and roughly an hour the first time.

The app's interface and voice prompts are in Ukrainian.

1

Install Xcode

Open the App Store on your Mac, find Xcode and install it. Launch it once: it asks to install extra components, agree. If it asks about platforms, choose iOS.

2

Add your Apple ID to Xcode

Xcode → Settings (⌘,) → Accounts tab → “+” at the bottom left → Apple ID → sign in.

3

Download the code

On the repository page, click the green Code button → Download ZIP. Double-click the archive to unpack it, for example into Downloads.

Download ZIP
4

Create the Xcode project

A small tool called XcodeGen generates the project file. Open Terminal (⌘Space → “Terminal”) and run the commands one at a time: copy, paste, press Return.

a. Install Homebrew, the package manager for Mac (skip if you have it). It asks for your Mac password; the characters don't show while you type, that's expected. If it ends with a Next steps section, run those commands too.

/bin/bash -c "$(curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Homebrew/install/HEAD/install.sh)"

b. Install XcodeGen:

brew install xcodegen

c. Type cd (with a space at the end), drag the StoneNavigationEmulation folder from the unpacked archive into the Terminal window, and press Return.

d. Generate and open the project:

xcodegen generate && open StoneNavigationEmulation.xcodeproj
5

Sign it with your Apple ID

  1. At the top left, click the blue StoneNavigationEmulation icon.
  2. Under TARGETS, pick StoneNavigationEmulation, then the Signing & Capabilities tab.
  3. Team: choose your name marked “(Personal Team)”.
  4. Bundle Identifier: replace com.example with something of your own, for example com.janedoe.StoneNavigationEmulation. It must be unique worldwide.

The red errors under Team should go away.

6

Prepare the iPhone

  1. Connect the iPhone with a cable, unlock it and tap Trust when asked about the computer.
  2. Turn on Developer Mode: Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode. The phone restarts; confirm afterwards.

If there's no Developer Mode item, it appears once the iPhone has seen Xcode (step 7). Come back here then.

7

Run it

  1. In the device list at the top middle of Xcode, pick your iPhone.
  2. Press ▶ (⌘R). The first build takes a few minutes.
  3. If the iPhone says Untrusted Developer: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust. Then press ▶ again or just open the app.

Stone Nav appears on your home screen. You don't need the Mac for driving.

8

First launch on the phone

  • Location: allow it, then choose Always. Otherwise navigation stops when the screen is off.
  • Motion & Fitness: allow it, so the app can tell stops and turns without GPS.
  • Ukrainian voice: if the app's Settings say it's missing: iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Ukrainian → download.

The signature lasts 7 days

With a free Apple ID such an app runs for 7 days and then stops opening. To extend it, connect the iPhone to the Mac, open StoneNavigationEmulation.xcodeproj in the same folder and press ▶ again. Settings, saved places and the last route are kept.

With the paid Apple Developer Program (99 USD a year) it lasts a year, and you can share the app through TestFlight.

Troubleshooting

  • “Couldn't load project "NavigationCore"” or “Missing package product”. Quit Xcode completely (⌘Q) and open the project again.
  • “Failed to register bundle identifier” or “No profiles for …”. That Bundle Identifier is taken. Change it, step 5.
  • “Developer Mode disabled”. Turn on Developer Mode, step 6.
  • Xcode doesn't see the phone. Unlock the iPhone, reconnect the cable and tap Trust again.
  • You downloaded a newer version. Repeat steps 4c–4d and 5 for the new folder.

For developers

To avoid picking the team in Xcode after every xcodegen generate, put your Team ID and bundle ID prefix in Config/Local.xcconfig. It isn't tracked by git.

cp Config/Local.xcconfig.example Config/Local.xcconfig

Navigation logic lives in the NavigationCore Swift package and is tested without a simulator: swift test. More in ARCHITECTURE.md.
